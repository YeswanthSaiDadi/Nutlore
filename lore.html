The Nutlore Lore

From our farm
to your table.

A short story of how a Nutlore ingredient moves from its beginning on our farms toward the finished food and blend customers enjoy.

The journey is part of the product.Grow → Harvest → Prepare → Craft → Pack → Table

The journey

Six steps.
One lore.

We keep the story straightforward: close to the ingredient, close to the work and clear about the journey.

01 — GROW

Our Farms

Our dry fruits begin on our own farms, where Nutlore stays connected to the ingredients from the start.

02 — HARVEST

Gather

Once ready, the ingredients move into the next stage of the Nutlore journey.

03 — PREPARE

Ready the ingredients

Ingredients are prepared for the formats they will become, with care given to their role in each product.

04 — CRAFT

Make the Bites & Blends

The ingredients become the foods and beverages that make up the Nutlore range.

05 — PACK

Prepare for the journey

Finished products are packed so they can move from our hands toward the people they are made for.

06 — TABLE

Enjoy

The journey reaches its final destination: the customer's table, routine and everyday moment.
